# Damage display: one number per move and creature (2026-10-23)

Status: current. It replaces the one-number-per-hit display of [handoff-2026-10-20](handoff-2026-10-20.md) item 2; the stacking rows and the number's style stay.

## Decision

The timing study for the original skill effects ([skill-effects-seamless-2026-10-22](skill-effects-seamless-2026-10-22.md)) found that the original fight module drew the damage number and moved the HP bar only when the effect ended, while the remake shows them with the target's hurt. The user's decision on 2026-10-23, verbatim:

> 就现在这样，但可以延长伤害显示时间，并且把多段伤害、额外伤害显示在一起，只有其他特殊的状态伤害是单独结算

- Keep the current timing: the number and the HP bar come with the target's hurt.
- The number may stay on screen longer.
- A move's multi-hit damage and its extra damage are one display for that move on that target.
- Only the other special status damage (statuses and similar effects that settle on their own, such as poison or burn at the turn's end) is still shown separately.

## The original

Read from the decompiled PetFightDLL_201308 (`UseSkillController`, `AttackValue`, `RemainHpManager`, `BaseFighterPropView`) and the pre-2013 module, which follows the same rules:

- **One number per move.** The server sends one `lostHP` per move (with `atkTimes`, 0 for a miss, and one crit flag). Each hit frame of the effect (`hit=1`) only plays the target's hurt; the number and the one HP-bar update come when the effect ends (`isEnd`). There is no per-hit or per-rider field: fixed and percentage extra damage of the move is inside `lostHP`.
- **Look.** 方正综艺 43 bold, dark red with a yellow glow, `-N` with a 克制/微弱/无效 prefix; `MISS` for a miss, no text for a status move that does 0. A crit draws a flaming digit picture instead.
- **The attacker's own loss.** The move's `gainHP` (the attacker's HP change from its own move, a dark red `-N` when negative) is drawn on the attacker at the same moment as the move's number, and its HP bar moves with the target's (`UseSkillController.as` 805-834, 870-877).
- **MISS.** `atkTimes` 0 gives `MISS` at the target's number spot, in the number's own style; this check comes before the status-move rule (`UseSkillController.as` 749-751 in PetFightDLL_201308, 342-344 in the pre-2013 module).
- **Blocks.** Nothing in the client names a blocked hit: the move's one `lostHP` is what got through, and the battle log (`msg_txt`) prints no damage and no block.
- **Lifetime.** It rises 15 px in 0.5 s, holds, and is removed at 1.5 s with no fade; the next move waits for that timer.
- **Status and turn-end damage** got no number per status. After both moves, `RemainHpManager` showed each pet's net HP change of the round as one magenta number at its usual damage spot, without holding up the next round.
- **Separate attacks.** Only 追击 follow-ups and the 合体 second strike had numbers of their own, each after its own animation.

## The rule

**Tallies** (`src/features/battle/battle-damage-tally.ts`). Within one dispatch's events, a move runs from its `move_used` until the next action, entry, tick or turn end (`turn_start`, `turn_end`, `item_used`, `capture_attempt`, `switch_out`, `switch_in`, `condition_tick`, `cant_act`, `no_target`, `flee`, `battle_end`). Inside a move, every `damage` event without a `status` joins the open tally of its creature (side and slot):

- on the target: every hit of a multi-hit move and the move's riders (`fixed_damage`, `pct_damage`, `ohko`, script riders through `hitFoe`, the `cond_bonus_fixed_*` buffs that deal their rider through an `on_hit` hook with skill 0, a forced execution);
- on the user, as its own tally: recoil, self costs and the counters the move draws (Boss `reflect_damage`, `cond_counter_1_k` such as 反击风暴, 思维干涉, 光之盾, 反射).

A `heal`, `revive`, `faint`, `transform` or `max_hp_changed` of the creature closes its tally, so a Boss's full heal, a life or a phase between two hits starts a new number. Status damage, damage after `turn_end`, at a turn's start, at an entry or from an item is a tally of one event.

**Engine marker.** A Boss's turn-end drain (`drain_each_turn`) or a 黑水吸附 tick comes straight after that Boss's own move damage, with the same source and nothing between; the `cond_bonus_fixed_*` riders carry skill 0. So neither the source nor the skill can tell a rider from turn-end damage. The engine now emits a textless `turn_end { turn }` at the top of `endOfTurn` (battle/RULES.md section 8). It changes no rule, draw or result.

**Frames.** Every hit keeps its frame, its hurt clip and its HP-bar step, so the turn is exactly as long as before, apart from the counters, blocks and misses below. A hit frame's `amount` is its tally's running total of `amount + overkill`, and a later frame of the tally names the first in `numberId`. `critical` stays the hit's own (its impact, the 致命一击 popup and the 800 ms hold). The tally's last frame carries the line of the total (`布布花受到 93 点伤害。剩余体力 7/100。`, with 致命一击 when any hit was critical); earlier frames carry none, so the move's line stays on screen while the hits land.

**The number.** It appears with the first hurt and counts up in place with each later one: it keeps its row, turns critical when any hit was, and is drawn again with a short swell. `DAMAGE_NUMBER_MS` (src/features/battle/battle-playback.ts) is 4000 ms from the number's last change, up from 3000 ms: the user asked for longer numbers and then chose 4 s over the first version's 4.5 s (2026-10-23); the original's 1.5 s is shorter than what the user asked for, so it does not set the value. Numbers never hold a frame. Other numbers on the same creature (a status tick, the next move) take the next free row, up to `DAMAGE_NUMBER_ROWS`. A hit that changes neither the amount nor the critical style (a 0-damage hit into an HP floor or a damage cap of 0) leaves the number as it is: it does not swell, and its time does not start again.

**A number stays on its creature.** Each number records the creature it is on (`FloatingNumber.uid`, src/features/battle/battle-player.ts). When another creature of its side enters (a switch frame), the numbers of the one it replaced leave with it, so the creature that comes in never shows the other's number and its own first number takes row 0. A transform is the same creature and keeps its numbers. Without this, the longer time kept a departed creature's number over its replacement for up to 2.6 s instead of 0.5–0.9 s (a 巴鲁斯 that switched out at once after taking −7, a 布布花 that fainted on recoil).

**The HP bar** keeps stepping down with each hurt. At every frame the number is the damage shown so far and the bar the HP left after it, so they never disagree; the lethal hit's overkill is in the number, and the bar stops at 0. Drain, leech, recoil, rewards and the journal keep the engine's real HP loss.

**Counters show with the hit that drew them.** A counter is damage on the move's user from the other side that follows a hit on the target with nothing between: a Boss's `reflect_damage` (肯佩德, 博卡特, 帕多尼) or an `on_hurt` reflection of the `cond_counter_1_k` buffs (反击风暴, 思维干涉, 光之盾, 反射); the tallies mark it (`TallyShare.drawnBy`). The presentation folds it into that hit's frame (`BattlePresentationFrame.counter`): the frame shows both HP bars after it and the attacker's number so far, and the line of the counters' tally, on its last counter, follows the hit's in that frame's dialogue (`布布花受到 40 点伤害。剩余体力 60/100。　巴鲁斯受到 10 点伤害。剩余体力 90/100。`). So the hits hand over as follow-up hits (`FOLLOW_UP_HIT_MS`) while the attacker is still at the target, the attacker's number and bar change with each hurt, and the counters hold no frame and play no hurt of their own, so the attacker's attack clip is not interrupted. Before, each hit was followed by a counter on the other creature, so every hit held `FRAME_MS.hit` and waited for the attacker's clip, and hits 2..n landed after it had gone home. The original drew the attacker's own loss from its move with the move's number (above). A counter after a line (a condition cured by the hit) keeps a frame of its own, as before, so the lines keep their order; recoil (no source) is not a counter and keeps its frame. A counter that takes the attacker to 0 HP now does so while its clip is still at the target: its bar and number show it at once, but the creature is greyed out only once its clip is home (`BattleCombatant` greys a creature at 0 HP unless it is acting its move), and the faint line follows as before. The first version greyed it at the target, a creature at 35% opacity still acting (博卡特's reflection taking a 巴鲁斯 at 10 HP to 0/309 in 乱突, and the foe's 乱突 into 反击风暴).

**Blocks are one line per move and creature** (`damageBlocks`). Within a move, the blocks on one side (`protected`, and `damage_blocked` for each hit or rider a Boss rule stops: `invulnerable_turns`, `damage_only_from`, `no_repeat_type`, `immune_fixed_damage`) are one line where the first came (for stopped counters, the last; see below), in the dialogue and the log; the others have no line and no frame. It reads `抵挡了这次伤害。` when none of the move's damage got through to that creature, and `抵挡了部分伤害。` when some did, so it never contradicts the move's damage line (鲁斯王's 浪打千击 into 哈莫雷特, whose `no_repeat_type` stops every hit after the first: `哈莫雷特受到 35 点伤害。剩余体力 9965/10000。　哈莫雷特抵挡了部分伤害。`). A block outside a move (a status tick a Boss ignores) stands alone as before. A damaging move stopped on its target before any of its damage reached it shows −0 there with that line (`BlockShare.stopped`, 2026-10-24, below). A rule of the attacker that stops a counter (`immune_fixed_damage` against 反击风暴's fixed damage, or `invulnerable_turns` and `damage_only_from`) is a counter too (`counterHits`, `BlockShare.drawnBy`): it folds into the hit that drew it, so the hits still hand over as follow-up hits, and the one block line is on the last of them, after the move's damage line in the last hit's dialogue, in the order the log has (`布布花受到 60 点伤害。剩余体力 40/100。　巴鲁斯抵挡了这次伤害。`). Before, its block line held a frame after the first hit, which waited for the attacker's clip, and the later hits landed after it had gone home. No catalog Boss has such a rule and a multi-hit move, so only scripted battles reach this. The original has no block wording to follow; it follows the original in showing one thing per move, with only what got through in the number.

**MISS.** A `miss` event is a `miss` frame on the move's target (`BattlePresentationFrame.side` is the target, though the event names the user) with the line `巴鲁斯的乱突没有命中。`. The move frame before it hands over at the clip's hit marker, or when its skill effect would hurt the target, as for a hit; the miss frame waits for the attacker's clip and effect like a lone hit and holds `FRAME_MS.miss`, the 480 ms that line held as a message before. It shows `MISS` (`FloatingNumber.kind` 'miss') in the number's style, a little smaller and spaced as a word, in the next free row of the target's stack, for `DAMAGE_NUMBER_MS`, and it never counts up. Its moment follows the user's choice of timing (the number comes with the hurt), where the original drew `MISS` at the effect's end. A missed status move shows `MISS` too: in the original the status-move rule (no text for a status move that does 0) is reached only when `atkTimes` is not 0, so a status move that lands has hits and one that misses has none and shows `MISS`. A multi-hit move cannot miss on a later hit here: the engine rolls accuracy once per move (`reachesTarget`, battle/core/src/engine/move.ts) and a multi-hit stops early only at a faint or an immunity (`免疫了这次攻击。`). The original would have shown such a move's landed total, since `MISS` needs `atkTimes` 0.

**The log** writes one line per tally, where its last damage came, with the total and the HP left after it (`巴鲁斯使用了三连击。　布布花受到 93 点伤害。剩余体力 7/100。`). The dialogue and the log take their lines from one function (`shownStepText`, src/features/battle/battle-log.ts), so the log reads them in the order the dialogue showed them, and a line that comes between a tally's hits reads before the total in both (2026-10-24, below). `turn_end` opens no log entry: the turn end's lines before any tick (a condition running out, a Boss's drain or regeneration, stage changes) join the last move's entry, as they did before the marker, and a condition tick opens its own as before. The first version of this change let `turn_end` open an entry; since the dialogue's recent log shows the last two entries, that left a move of the turn out of it in 140 of 1047 swept turns (13.4%, against 75 or 7.2% before), often the player's own damage total, so it was taken back. The tallies close at `turn_end` on their own and do not need the log entry.

## Kept apart from the original on purpose

- Status and turn-end damage show one number and one line per event, as before, not one net number per pet per round.
- The number comes with the first hurt and counts up, instead of appearing once at the effect's end (the user kept the current timing).
- A life, a full heal or a phase between two hits of one move splits its damage on that target into several numbers and lines (a 3-hit move into a Boss with two lives shows −41, −42 and −45), so that the number never runs past what the HP bar shows. This is the one exception to one number per move and target; the user kept it when asked (2026-10-23).

## Follow-ups (2026-10-24)

The three damage-display open points of [handoff-2026-10-22](handoff-2026-10-22.md).

**A line between a tally's hits.** A line that comes between a tally's hits (卡布尔获得每回合附加50点固定伤害效果 between 刺身撞击's hit and its 50 rider, 致命一击破防 between a critical hit and its rider, a block between two hits) read before the total in the dialogue and after it in the log, which wrote the total where the first damage came. The log now writes the total where the last damage came, so both read `巴鲁斯使用了龙之怒。　致命一击破防，布布花的防御提升已清除。　布布花受到 80 点伤害，致命一击！剩余体力 20/100。`. That is the order the player sees: while the hits land the number counts up and the bar steps down, and the line between them shows in the dialogue then; the total's line names the HP left after the last hurt, so it can only come with the number's and the bar's last change. Moving the dialogue's total to the first hit instead would print 80 and 20/100 while the number still read 40 and the bar 60/100. 致命一击破防 now reads before the total when the critical hit had a rider after it; after a multi-hit it still follows the total, since the engine clears the defence after the last hit (`clearCriticalDefence`, battle/core/src/engine/move.ts).

**A fully blocked damaging move shows −0.** The evidence holds on a second reading of both fight modules: `onMovieOver` draws `MISS` when `atkTimes` is 0, no text for a status move (category 4) whose `lostHP` is 0, and otherwise `effectName + "-" + lostHP` (PetFightDLL_201308 `UseSkillController.as` 749-768, the pre-2013 module 342-353). Nothing else in the client stops a number, so a damaging move that reached its target and did 0 drew `-0` (with 克制 or 微弱 before it, or a grey `无效-0`); a block has no field of its own (`AttackValue` reads `atkTimes`, `lostHP`, `realHurtHp`, `gainHP`, `remainHp`). What the client cannot show is the server's `atkTimes` for a block: with 0 it would have drawn `MISS`. The original's effect texts say which is which, and the engine follows them: an effect that makes attacks miss says so (`{0}回合内{category}攻击对自身必定miss`, also where its flavour text says 免疫) and is a miss here (`evade`, battle/core/src/scripts/builders.ts) that shows MISS, while one that cancels damage (圣光门's 免疫当回合伤害, 暗之反射) lets the hit land at 0 (`modify_damage_final`), which already showed `−0` here. The blocks that stop a whole move are the Boss rules that let a hit land and stop its damage (`damage_blocked` for `invulnerable_turns`, `damage_only_from`, `no_repeat_type`, `immune_fixed_damage`), the second kind; no catalog skill uses the `protect` op behind `protected`. So the remake draws the `-0` of such a move: its block line's frame (`BlockShare.stopped` in `damageBlocks`, `BattlePresentationFrame.stopped`) shows `−0` on the target, in the next free row, for `DAMAGE_NUMBER_MS`, and keeps the line `哈莫雷特抵挡了这次伤害。`. It is not drawn for a status move (no text in the original), for a move whose rider or 0-damage hit reached the target (that tally's number already shows what got through), for a stopped counter (the original drew the attacker's own loss only when `gainHP` was not 0) or for a block outside a move. The number comes with the block line, when the attacker's clip and effect have ended: a block plays no hurt for a number to come with, that is the original's moment (`isEnd`), and so every turn keeps its planned time. It names the stopped hit's type rating since the type ratings below.

**The team strip waits for the greying.** A creature that a counter takes to 0 HP while its clip is still at the target is greyed out only once it is home (above), but the team strip under its HP bar counted it out at once: the frame's party has it at 0 HP, and the strip read `hp > 0`. Both now read one rule (`shownDown` and `isActing` in src/features/battle/battle-playback.ts): a creature at 0 HP is down once it is not acting its move, where acting is the move whose clip still plays on its side or, without a clip, the move frame on screen. `BattleCombatant` greys with it and `BattleTeamStrip` counts the creature in battle out with it (its count, its × mark, 无法战斗 in its details), so both turn at the same moment, when the clip is home; until then the strip's details show its HP as `HP 0/309`, as the bar does. Without a loaded clip the creature is down as soon as the hit shows, as before, since nothing acts then.

**Checks (2026-10-24).** Unit tests: `battle-damage-tally.test.mjs` (a critical hit, its 破防 line and its rider, and a block between two hits, read in the log as the dialogue shows them; −0 for three blocked hits and for a protection, and not for a rider that got through, a 0-damage hit, a status move, a stopped counter or a block outside a move), `battle-player.test.mjs` (the stopped move's −0 shows with its block line when the clip has ended, in the next free row, for `DAMAGE_NUMBER_MS`) and `battle-playback.test.mjs` (`sideAction`, `isActing`, `shownDown`). **The original**, read again in both fight modules (decompiled with FFDec; the sources are not in the repository):

So a neutral move has no prefix, and with one `lostHP` per move a multi-hit move has one prefix on its one number. The client rates nothing itself: how the server classed a dual-type multiplier (×4, ×1.5, ×1.25, ×0.75, ×0.25; battle/RULES.md section 6) is not in it.

**The rule** (battle/RULES.md section 5):

- **Which number.** A move's number on its target names the move's rating, from the type multiplier of the move's first hit the chart rated: the engine's `damage.effectiveness`, kept on the tally (`DamageTally.effectiveness`, `damageTallies` in src/features/battle/battle-damage-tally.ts). Every frame of the number carries it (`BattlePresentationFrame.typeEffect`, `FloatingNumber.typeEffect`), so it reads 克制−30, then 克制−61 as it counts up; a rider that joins a rated hit's number shares its rating. A move whose damage the chart did not rate (fixed, percentage or true damage alone) names none: the engine attaches no multiplier to it, and the server's code for it is not in the client.
- **Classes.** One rule rates the numbers and the matchup indicator beside the HP bars (`matchupRelation` and `typeEffect` in src/features/battle/type-matchup.ts, labels from `MATCHUP_LABELS`): ×0 is 无效, below ×1 微弱, above ×1 克制 (also ×1.25 and ×1.5 of a dual type), ×1 nothing. For the dual-type multipliers this is the remake's reading, since the server's class for them is not in the client.
- **A stopped move.** The −0 of a damaging move a Boss rule stopped entirely names its first stopped hit's rating: the engine's `damage_blocked` event now carries the hit's `effectiveness` (battle/core/src/engine/damage.ts; `BlockShare.effectiveness` in `damageBlocks`). A water move into a fire creature that only fire hurts shows 克制−0 with `小火猴抵挡了这次伤害。` (damage-presentation). A `protected` move is stopped before the chart rates it, so its −0 names none.
- **Type immunity.** A damaging move its target's type is immune to (the engine's `immune`, `免疫了这次攻击。`, emitted only for an attack before any of its damage) shows 无效−0 on the target with that line (`immuneMoves`), as a stopped move's −0 shows with its block line: in the line's frame, in the next free row, for `DAMAGE_NUMBER_MS`. The line already had its frame, so every turn keeps its planned time and the dialogue and the log are as before. An `immune` after a hit of the move, or outside a move, shows nothing.
- **None.** Counters and recoil on the move's user, status damage, turn-end damage and every other number outside a move's tally on its target name no rating; a number that turns critical drops its prefix, as the original's flaming digits had none.
- **Look.** 克制 and 微弱 are drawn in the number's own style, as the original drew its prefix in the number's format (the number's style is the remake's, `classic-damage-number`). 无效−0 is #666 with a white stroke where the original had the white glow, and the dark drop shadow #2a2d32 of the matchup indicator's 无效 (`.classic-damage-number.immune`, src/features/battle/ui/battle.css).
