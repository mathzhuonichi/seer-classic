# Classic navigation and presentation correction

Version 0.3.2 implements the follow-up interaction and art corrections. The source remains a development delivery at port 1421; no native packaging or frontend production build was performed.

## Changes

Non-capturable encounters now omit the capsule category completely. The names in the combat health panels are retained, while duplicate badges below the portraits are removed. Capture uses a cylindrical capsule and an ordered 2.2-second presentation: visible arrival, opening/draw-in, closure/shake, then the actual outcome. Result text and stars are delayed until the outcome portion. The global toast is suppressed inside combat so settlement cannot announce success before the presentation reaches it. Reduced-motion playback retains this ordering while limiting movement.

Wild creatures turn with an immediate horizontal mirror, without perspective rotations or an interpolation through a flat side. Each roaming leg chooses a random destination, speed and pause; legs can be straight or quadratic curves. Their endpoints and control points stay within bounds measured from the complete portrait/name host. Selected creatures remain fixed for approach and dialogue. Scene windows, combat and hidden documents pause the routes.

The player now uses eight genuine original Seer directional composites. Original colour, face/detail and default equipment layers were recovered from official SWFs and assembled at their original registrations. Roger and Pat use the equipment and colours recorded in the official NPC configuration. The extraction, hashes and standing-frame mappings are recorded in `public/robots/classic/manifest.json` and `tools/assets/README.md`.

The navigation window now provides galaxy selection, planet selection, landing-region selection and an always-visible confirmation footer. It supports Pano, Kalan and Rogue, 13 destinations including the ship, and 39 landing regions. The galaxy/name memberships were checked against the official RobotCoreDLL's embedded galaxy XMLs. Existing and new destinations use the project's authored scenery; additional planet locations explicitly reuse existing themes. Planet and region lists scroll independently inside the game window.

## Content boundaries

The character sprites are recovered original vectors, with the original configurable player colour selected for this remake. Stationary NPCs have one standing direction. The added planet scenes are authored theme reuse, not newly recovered original background art. The three selectable galaxies and listed destinations are the implemented subset; this does not imply that every historical Seer galaxy or planet is present. The previously agreed fifth-skill, soul-mark and engraving data interfaces remain in place.

## Character and damage follow-up

The reported incomplete player and overlapping captain revealed an assembly error: the previous compositor placed foot equipment above the body and did not reconstruct every nested source matrix. All eight player views and both NPCs now follow the original standing-frame display lists and direction-specific depth order. The full placement audit remains under the ignored asset cache; the public manifest records provenance and its audit hash. See [the assembly diagnosis](../../public/robots/classic/ASSEMBLY.md). The application also excludes the character from name-label styling, constrains NPC grid rows to their assigned height, and uses output hashes in image URLs to replace stale artwork.

The damage corrections and evidence boundaries are documented in [the source research](damage-formula-research.md) and [the implementation audit](damage-implementation-audit.md). Twenty-eight focused damage-chain cases passed, compared with twelve failures in the saved baseline. Eight further critical-hit cases passed, covering corresponding post-damage defense-stage clearing, preserved stages, blocked/missed hits, explicit immunity and the documented multi-hit boundary. Project TypeScript checking and diff checks passed. These tests verify the reconstruction's declared behavior, not an exact unrecovered server routine.

The supported live browser showed complete player, captain and scientist sprites, switched to an original side-facing player image after walking, and fitted the scientist correctly in both the scene and dialogue. A normal wild encounter loaded through the progress screen and resolved one attack: the enemy lost 5 HP (16 to 11), the player lost 3 HP (21 to 18), and the grouped log reported those same values. Retreat ended the battle without rewards and returned to the scene; the verification party was restored through the regular recovery window. The combat observation is retained at `artifacts/damage-v6/live-battle.txt`. Three GPT-6.1-sol contributors with high reasoning effort completed the asset, source-research and independent implementation work. Development previews remain available on ports 1421 and 1424; no production bundle or native package was built.

## Map population follow-up

The redundant right-hand interstellar transporter and its dialogue branch are removed. The existing map menu and adjacent-region exits remain available. Habitats with ordinary wild species now contain six creatures distributed across two rows, using only their existing species lists. Empty habitats receive no additions. Xiao Dou Ya, Mobi, Link and Bulu remain singular as a conservative display-density policy; this is not a reconstruction of historical spawn probabilities. Every instance has a stable scene-specific identity, so approaching one creature pauses that individual while its same-species neighbours continue their independent bounded routes.

Focused population assertions and project TypeScript checking passed. The supported live browser verified six Pipi in Klose grassland, five cactus creatures plus one Xiao Dou Ya in the swamp, and no wild additions in the boss-only forest. Only the approached Pipi paused during dialogue; its neighbours kept roaming. All six sampled swamp creature hosts stayed inside the 1280-by-720 viewport's scene, all image resources loaded, and the transporter count was zero. The retained top map menu completed the galaxy/planet/landing workflow to Cloud Planet, where five Mao Mao and one Mobi also loaded within the scene bounds. Evidence is retained under the ignored `artifacts/population-v10/` directory. One existing GPT-6.1-sol contributor with high reasoning effort implemented the population helper; integration and live interaction checks were performed in the main task. No frontend production bundle or native package was built.
