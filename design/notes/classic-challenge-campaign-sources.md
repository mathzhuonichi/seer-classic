# Classic challenge campaigns: source and implementation record

Seven campaign definitions now describe the original encounter order for the four divine beasts, Qilin, Rei's skill training, and Puni's seals followed by its body. Seventeen new encounter records provide the missing beast and Qilin battles. Guardian groups, avatar groups, and multi-life bodies are represented by one ordered enemy team, with revival phases where necessary. A new opponent within that encounter does not open a healing or team-management checkpoint.

This is a source-backed reconstruction of the flows, not a recovered original server implementation. Each new JSON retains its sources, uncertain fields, and explicit numeric calibration. Original client code supplies stronger evidence for identity, entry checks, and dialogue; period first-person walkthroughs and their screenshots supply battle order and observed mechanics. Later summaries are kept separate from release-era reports. Pre-release Qilin predictions, later complete-form challenges, and spinoff mechanics were excluded.

## Original-client evidence

The official [RobotAppDLL.swf](https://seer.61.com/dll/RobotAppDLL.swf) is referenced by the cached official `config/dll.xml` and loaded by `Client.swf`'s `net/DLLLoader.as`. Decoding skips the seven-byte loader prefix and inflates the remaining payload. The encoded file's SHA-256 is `517e14539880312aa5cdbbe083ebb6b4af3d171ef6a914fd026c00d5bb55e960`; the decoded file's SHA-256 is `c6493fbaf34a60f378b8badebb5426172f6d08b5ad99e4bd27b4f13f5bc95544`. Both files and extracted scripts are retained under ignored `.cache/campaign-sources/`.

The source hashes and retained controller paths are also recorded in `.cache/campaign-sources/manifest.json`. The focused source inspection used these extracted classes:

- `com/robot/app/sptStar/LeiyiTrainController.as` checks Rei as the first selected creature, tracks the five special-skill IDs, and explicitly names Rainy Day, the finishing use of Extreme Electric Thousand Birds, and ten rounds followed by a knockout while Akexiya is paralyzed. The current retrieved client also recognizes Rei's later evolution, so the snapshot is not misrepresented as an untouched 2010 binary.
- `com/robot/app/sptGalaxy/XuanWuController.as` lists six ordinary SPT prerequisites, checks their task completion, and implements the randomly placed portal during minutes 50–59. Its `check(true)` path bypasses those prerequisites. This does not establish the server statistics of the six in-space guardian variants.
- `com/robot/app/sptGalaxy/ZhuQueController.as` checks Delasa, Kaxius, and Difute, and lists a timed random portal. The later statue route is a distinct version of access.
- The previously recovered official `PetXMLInfo.as` associates ID 505 with `openQilin`. The existing catalog names this species 阿瑞斯; it is Qilin, not species 1404 or 1405. Species 501–504 are Bastet, Dorag, Tiger, and Phoenix. No species substitution or new species record was required.

The broad script export was stopped after the required controllers were recovered. Some unrelated methods timed out during decompilation; no claim of exhaustive client recovery is made. The damage packet parsing boundary recorded in [the damage implementation audit](damage-implementation-audit.md) still applies: client dialogue does not recover server damage calculations.

`CHALLENGE_CAMPAIGNS` in `src/progression/challenge-campaigns.ts` is the integration contract. Every stage references exactly one encounter ID. `kind: 'chain'` identifies a multi-opponent or reviving encounter; `carry: true` describes continuity within that encounter. Campaign-level `continuity: 'checkpoint'` keeps distinct challenge stages separate. `resetOnLoss` is omitted where the historical campaign-wide failure boundary was not recovered; it is explicitly false for Phoenix's persistent guardian-clear flow. A failure inside an uninterrupted guardian battle requires replaying that battle from its first opponent. Persistence of other previously completed stages is an integration policy where the sources do not settle it, not a newly asserted historical fact.

The lake scene and dedicated campaign scenes are authored presentation layouts. Timed portals, random appearance, legacy prerequisites, and subscription-assisted preparation are recorded above rather than silently imposed on the existing anytime-access remake. The recovered encounter's Akexiya minimum level 80 is retained in the integration definition; the inspected controller confirms the ten-round/paralysis condition but does not independently establish that level gate. The first four Rei training battles were historically selectable training tasks; presenting them as a fixed sequence follows the user's requested campaign experience, while the reflection's prerequisite of all four skills is sourced.

## Numeric variants and repaired catalog records

The new guardian records use current catalog species and skills. Their level 100, IV 31, zero EVs, neutral nature, unspecified defense/speed scaling, and most guardian HP values are explicitly calibrated. They are stronger guardian variants, not falsely relabeled copies of the weak isolated SPT fights. All cited numeric values remain attached to the applicable dated variant.

The [August 2011 beast discussion](https://news.4399.com/seer/jingyanxinde/201108-18-110516.html), by 星痕, reports 50,000 HP for Bastet and Dorag, initial attack/special-attack boosts respectively, and Dorag's 1,000 HP recovery per turn. It also locates the four beasts at Phoenix's forbidden area after the August update. The White Tiger walkthrough reports 50,000 HP for its body. Phoenix's body HP 50,000, the two White Tiger avatars' HP 10,000, and the light shadow's HP 2,000 are calibration, not recovered original numbers. Exact low-HP speed triggers and source-specific fatigue exceptions remain incomplete.

The January Qinglong guide reports retaliation at damage **at least 200** for its Eersaila guardian. Other period guides report a threshold above 150. The new record explicitly selects the dated 200-inclusive variant and retains the conflict. Physical-only status retaliation is represented with explicit category filters. The White Tiger fighting avatar adds one priority step, as the March guide states; it does not receive an absolute-first override. Named forbidden instant-KO skills are distinguished from permitted instant-kill traits; a blanket OHKO immunity would incorrectly remove historically valid strategies.

The October 4, 2013 Qilin body guide reports six named-life HP values **20,000 / 15,000 / 15,000 / 10,000 / 15,000 / 50,000**, followed by a **15,000 HP** reactivation of the last life. Six ordered members plus the last member's revival implement this as one encounter within the six-member limit. The same page's table says a +12 boost after rebirth, while its actual walkthrough says +6; +6 is selected and the conflict is preserved. Later 2014 summaries give 8,000 HP for the first life and are not substituted into this 2013 variant. First-wheel HP 20,000 and second-wheel HP 15,000 are separately reported in 2013. Third-through-sixth wheel HP 15,000 is retained as calibration from later summaries.

Qilin's fixed-damage wheels, intrinsic-priority/ramping body filters, critical-only fourth wheel/body, above-1,000-damage fifth-body retaliation, and final revival use explicit rule parameters. Fixed damage is **not** silently allowed in critical-only mode: the recovered description establishes critical attacks, but not that exception. The first body's low-damage trigger threshold of 1,000 is calibrated because the source does not quantify it. Counter-type direction, the first eligible use of a ramping move, and forced-status exceptions are also unverified reconstruction boundaries.

Authorized repairs to existing records are narrowly scoped:

- Training 1 now requires finishing with skill 10170, White Blade.
- Training 2 now requires using skill 20086, Rainy Day.
- Training 3 now requires finishing with skill 10175 and rewards **10824, 元气电光球**.
- Training 4 rewards **20364, 雷神觉醒**. The former reconstruction had the training 3 and 4 rewards swapped; its existing ten-round and paralysis objectives are preserved.
- The reflection selects the original-era **four-round / 1,200 HP** variant from the [2011 report](https://news.7k7k.com/content/20110316/76887.html). Later guides describe five rounds, so that discrepancy is recorded rather than merged into an undated rule. Its exact randomized IV/nature behavior remains calibrated.
- Puni's sixth body life is corrected from **6,500 to 65,000 HP** using the [October 2010 report](https://news.7k7k.com/content/20101028/18173.html), corroborated by period reports of more than 60,000 HP. Its below-1,000 recovery threshold is retained. Conflicting existing second, third, and fifth-life HP values are flagged for separate reconciliation; they were not silently changed in this patch.

Guardian, seal, avatar, wheel, and training stages must award progress or their actual skill rewards rather than automatically granting the final creature's essence. Only a completed body encounter grants that campaign's essence. The game-adapter owner implements the runtime progress and reward policy; the battle owner implements per-member rules, phases, types, revival, predicates, and the required campaign scripts. This record does not claim that data initialization alone verifies those mechanics or the final UI.

## Verification boundary

Focused record checks verify stage-to-encounter references, all species and skill IDs, one encounter per stage, guardian team order, final revival phases, Rei reward IDs and success predicates, and the repaired Puni HP. Project TypeScript checking passed after the definitions were written. The battle owner separately reported successful focused initialization of all seventeen new encounters with no unknown-rule warnings, and checks for local member rules, carried state, avatar types, retaliation, revival PP, and Qilin damage filters. Those reports remain distinct from a full original-server comparison or end-to-end campaign completion. No production build, native package, player-save reset, or legacy test-suite rerun was performed.
