# Critical-rate trait calibration

The original checked-in client records identify zero-star 会心 (ID 1023, effect 30) and 免爆 (ID 1063, effect 64), both with Args `1 0`. Their descriptions state an increase or decrease in critical-hit probability without an explicit denominator. Later upgrade records use different effect IDs and parameters; they do not establish the probability of the original zero-star tier.

A targeted search of Taomee's official site did not recover a numerical probability mapping for those two original trait effects. The official [Seer item catalogue](https://seer.61.com/main/props/NONO/index.html) specifies a 1/16 critical-hit increase for the critical energy bead. That supports the existence of a sixteen-part critical-rate increment in the classic product, but it does not establish that either trait uses the same increment.

This remake explicitly calibrates 会心 as one additional step on the engine's sixteen-point critical-rate scale, so the usual rate changes from 1/16 to 2/16. The mid-2010 client pet-effect file (BaiSugar/kose_seer `config/data/xml/skill_effects.xml`) now also supports it: 「30. 所有技能的致命一击率增加1/16」. A forced critical attack remains forced, and encounter rules that grant critical immunity retain priority. No later-star mapping is substituted.

免爆 no longer changes the critical rate (user decision 2026-10-21, [classic-traits-2026-10-21](classic-traits-2026-10-21.md)). Effect 64 is not in the 2010 file; 赛学机制论 必修6 (Bilibili cv25902667) says 「免爆实际为当回合免疫受到的暴击伤害（对暴击进行一个挡伤效果），而不是降低暴击概率」, and its table of star rates gives 6.25% for the zero star. A critical hit from the foe's attack on a 免爆 holder therefore deals 0 damage with chance 1/16, and the ordinary 1/16 rate applies to it again. Before that decision, 免爆 subtracted one incoming step, which made the usual rate 0.

These are playable remake choices, not verified historical trait probabilities. The raw original records remain intact. The public trait descriptions state the numeric behavior, and each of the two runtime traits keeps an English calibration note. This distinction should remain visible until a reliable original probability mapping is recovered.

The focused deterministic battle check (`tools/verification/battle-v3.ts`) verifies ordinary random critical damage separately from the traits, confirms that the additional-rate hook uses one step, and checks 免爆 with the chance forced: a zeroed critical hit keeps the holder's defence boost, a failed chance lets the critical damage through, and a 1/16 skill still crits against the holder. It does not certify equivalence with the original client battle server.
