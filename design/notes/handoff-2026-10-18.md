| Variant vs V57 | Score | Switch rate | Re-switch within 2 turns | Decision p50 / p95 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H=2, no gate | 65.4% [60.4, 70.1] | 9.5% | 135 | 124 / 204 ms |
| H=5, no gate | 65.3% [60.2, 70.0] | 9.5% | 131 | 125 / 228 ms |
| H=2, `SWITCH_GAP=2` (adopted) | 66.8% [61.8, 71.5] | 5.5% | 0 | 91 / 176 ms |

`ALIVE` 0.2–0.5, `RACE` 0.25–0.8 and `H` 1–5 do not change strength measurably. A larger horizon barely matters, because rollouts stop when the opponent's active monster faints, usually within 1–2 turns. About half of the voluntary switches were pure choices with a large evaluation gap; the likely causes are the flat `ALIVE` value of a near-dead monster, and rollouts in which the opponent never switches. Neither cause is verified yet.
