# Wild monsters that flee (2026-10-24)

Status: current. User decision 2026-10-24 (option c of question 6 in
[period-evidence-2026-10-24](period-evidence-2026-10-24.md), recorded in `handoff-2026-10-22.md`): wild 该伊, 尼尔 and 尼奥
flee as in the period original. The rule is `battle/RULES.md` 9.1; the data is each species' `wild_flee` in `data/monsters/`
(`data/README.md`). Checks: `battle/core/src/engine/wild-flee.test.mjs` and `tools/verification/battle-commands.mjs`; for 尼奥
also `capture-official-v25`, `habitat-regions`, `recruitment-acquisition`, `export-acquisition-status` and `skins`.

## 1. When 该伊 and 尼尔 flee

该伊 (474) has `wild_flee {from_turn: 5}` and 尼尔 (77) `{from_turn: 10}`: from that battle turn on, the wild monster flees at
its own action, in place of the move it chose and at its place in the turn order. It never flees in a turn it cannot act.

**Sources.**

- 4399 2010-12-07, [仙人掌抓五回合逃跑精灵方法测试](https://news.4399.com/seer/jingyanxinde/201012-07-82196.html), tested on 该伊:
  「1、先上波克尔，用手下留情使要抓的精灵只剩一滴血；2、换上巨型仙人掌；3、仙人掌用香甜气息；4、再用香甜气息；5、仙人掌用催眠粉；
  6、放胶囊 然后就是5、6两个步骤轮流使用直到抓住精灵。」 and 「确实，该伊是不会逃跑了」.
- Zhidao 2011-08-13, [怎么捉到该伊](https://zhidao.baidu.com/question/304347806.html): 「值得注意的是，该伊5回合后会逃跑。」
- 7k7k 2010-04-16, [野生精灵大全](https://news.7k7k.com/content/20100416/44585.html), 尼尔: 「会变身的超能系精灵。现在改到10回合才逃跑。
  已经十分罕见。」
- 7k7k 2011-02-09, [防止到手后逃跑](https://news.7k7k.com/content/20110209/62131.html) (the Spring Festival pets, out of scope,
  but the same mechanism): 「上谱尼或该隐灵魂干涉，疲惫后两个胶囊，之后接着灵魂干涉，两个胶囊 这样下来，精灵就不会逃跑了。」 and
  「如果你的灵魂干涉一旦MISS掉了精灵就会逃跑掉哦！」
- Later, not used: Zhidao 2012-05-27 ([429505505](https://zhidao.baidu.com/question/429505505.html)) 「尼尔现在8回合左右后会逃跑，
  要注意，不能打持久仗。」; Zhidao 2013-09-03 ([588268672](https://zhidao.baidu.com/question/588268672.html)) 「记住他会5回合逃跑」 and
  「五回合还是十回合会逃跑」.

**Reading.**

- *The flee is the monster's action.* Sleep (4399 2010) and 疲惫 (7k7k 2011) stop it, and a miss of the control move lets it
  flee at once, so it is not a turn-end event: it takes the monster's action. A faster foe's move comes first. 混乱 has no
  source; the remake lets a confused monster flee, since the flee is not a move that can fail.
- *Which turn.* 「五回合逃跑」 and 「5回合后会逃跑」 read as: at its action in turn 5. The 4399 method spends turns 1 to 4 on
  手下留情, the switch and two 香甜气息, and lands 催眠粉 in turn 5, before 该伊 acts: 巨型仙人掌 (base speed 80) outspeeds 该伊
  (56). A flee from turn 6 would leave turn 5 free and the method would not need its sleep there. 「现在改到10回合才逃跑」 reads
  the same way for 尼尔, at turn 10.
- *Turns it could not act count.* The guides count the battle's 回合, the round counter on the battle screen, and warn
  against long fights (「不能打持久仗」); none counts the monster's own actions. So the count is the battle's turn number: a monster
  kept asleep through turn n flees at its first action once it wakes. In the 4399 method the two readings agree (it acts in
  turns 1 to 4 and never again), so this is an inference from the wording, not a test.
- *Why 10 for 尼尔, not 8.* The 7k7k value is the earliest dated and says the count was changed to 10 (「现在改到」); the 2012
  「8回合左右」 is approximate and two years later, and the 2013 answers disagree with each other (5 or 10). The user chose the
  2010 count. 该伊's 5 agrees across 2010 and 2011.
- Only a wild side flees; a Boss or trainer of the same species fights on (none is fielded today).

## 2. The end of the battle and the screen

- **Result.** `winner: null`, `reason: wild_fled`, no capture, an empty `defeated`: no EXP, 学习力, coins, pooled experience or
  items, and no won battle. HP and PP carry over as after any battle; the move it chose in the flee turn spends no PP. Nothing
  in the save changes shape, so there is no migration.
- **Screen.** The `wild_fled` event is one `flee` frame on its side: the creature turns away and runs off the arena, and stays
  gone through the turn's closing frame and after it. The log line is 「该伊逃跑了！」; the result title 精灵逃跑了; the result text,
  which the scene's notice repeats once the battle closes, is the original's 「很可惜，这只精灵逃跑了！」. Source: the fight module
  `petFightModule_201308` of the live `PetFightDLL_201308.swf` (decoded 2026-10-24 for the period-evidence note):
  `FightOverController` maps `FightOverInfo.reason` 5 to `isNpcEscape` and shows that text.
- **Sound.** On that branch the client calls `SoundController.playEndMusic(true)`, the same call as a win. The remake plays no
  end music, as for a retreat and a draw (user decision 2026-10-24).

## 3. 尼奥 (416)

Until now the remake kept the live official client's capsule gate (`capture-official-v25`): with an active species outside
`[77,78,79,310,311,312]` the capsule could not be thrown at a wild 尼奥. The period game let the capsule go and 尼奥 fled.

**Sources.**

- 7k7k 2010-08-03, [尼奥捕捉指南](https://news.7k7k.com/content/20100803/11999.html): 「不管你的首选精灵是什么，进入对战发现是尼奥以后，
  必须把精灵换成尼尔（或者是尼尔的进化、闪尼、闪尼的进化），就用尼尔捉尼奥。如果你换了别的精灵，尼奥就会马上逃跑的。不换，捉到明天它都不会跑。」
- 4399 2010-08-02, [捕捉尼奥的心得和方法](https://news.4399.com/seer/jingyanxinde/201008-02-74588.html): 「用其他精灵来捕捉的话尼奥会逃跑，
  只有用尼尔来捕捉才不会逃走。」 The guide then leads with a Lv 20 菲斯利 (「首先直接首发一只20级左右的菲斯利」), since 尼尔 has no 手下留情.
- The partner list is the live client's (`CatchPetItemCategory.useItem`, `capture-official-v25`): 尼尔, 菲斯利, 艾斯菲亚, 闪光尼尔,
  闪光菲斯利, 闪光艾菲亚.
- The farewell: `FightOverController` of the live `PetFightDLL_201308.swf` (module `petFightModule_201308`), after a reason-5
  end against `petID == 416`: `NpcDialog.show(NPC.NIAO, ["我是来找我弟弟的，你看见他了吗？"], ["你弟弟到底是谁呢？好像我记得有只精灵和你长得很像。"])`.
  The first array is the NPC's line and the second the player's answer, as in the 2013-07-06 `RobotAppDLL`'s
  `CoreFightCmdListener` (`NpcDialog.show(NPC.LEIYI, ["速度才是最强的武器！"], ["有了雷伊的帮助，我一定能赢！"], [startFight])`). The
  answer has no handler, so it only closes the dialogue. `NPC.NIAO` is already an NPC id in the 2013-07-06 `RobotCoreDLL`, but
  the two lines are found only in the live build, which also holds 2016 and 2023 classes; the source does not date them.

**Decisions (user, 2026-10-24) and readings.**

- `wild_flee {unless_player_active: [77, 78, 79, 310, 311, 312]}`: 尼奥 flees at its own action in any turn in which the
  player's active monster is none of them, from turn 1. With one of them out it never flees and is caught like any monster.
- A switch resolves before its action, so the 7k7k plan works: a non-partner lead that switches to 尼尔 in turn 1 keeps it.
  Switching away from a partner makes it flee at its action in that same turn (「如果你换了别的精灵，尼奥就会马上逃跑的」).
- A capsule thrown with no partner out makes it flee at once, before any roll (option b, 4399's 「用其他精灵来捕捉的话尼奥会逃跑」).
  *Reading:* the capsule is thrown, so it is spent as a failed one would be (no source says either way; the engine spends every
  thrown capsule). In the game the capsules are unlimited (`kind: "capsule"`), so only an engine side with stock sees it.
  The capsule flee does not wait for 尼奥 to be able to act: asleep or 疲惫 it still flees, so only a partner can catch it, as
  both guides say. Its log line is 「我方使用了普通精灵胶囊，尼奥逃跑了！」, one `flee` frame with no capsule throw.
- The check reads the active monster's species in battle, not its skin (`skins` checks a 79 skin on another species).
- After the flee the battle's result is the same as section 2. Once the battle closes, the scene shows 尼奥's farewell
  through the world dialogue: its portrait and name, 「我是来找我弟弟的，你看见他了吗？」, and the player's answer
  「你弟弟到底是谁呢？好像我记得有只精灵和你长得很像。」 as the one button, which closes it (`wild_flee.farewell`, carried by the battle
  view's `farewell`). No end music.
- Deleted with the gate: `NIAO_CAPTURE_PARTNERS` and `meetsWildCapturePrerequisite` (`battle/core/src/capture.ts`), the
  legal-action check in `engine/actions.ts`, the view's refusal text 「捕捉尼奥需要派出…」, the world dialogue's hint
  「捕捉时需派出…」 and the acquisition module's `NIAO_SPECIES`. The acquisition route keeps `requiredActiveSpecies`, now read from
  `wild_flee.unless_player_active`: 尼奥 still cannot be obtained before a partner can, and the dex label says why
  (「需先获得并派出…中的任一形态，派出其他精灵时它会逃跑」).
