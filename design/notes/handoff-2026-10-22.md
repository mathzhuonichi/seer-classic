## Damage display (user decision 2026-10-23)

The user kept the current timing (the number and the HP bar come with the target's hurt, not at the effect's end as in the original) and asked for longer numbers, with a move's multi-hit and extra damage shown together and only special status damage settled on its own. Asked after the first version, the user chose 4 s for the numbers (it had 4.5 s) and kept the split where a life, a full heal or a phase between two hits of one move starts a new number, so that the number never runs past the HP bar. The user then asked to fix the three open points: counter timing, block lines and the missing MISS popup.

- **Landed** (4ae1138..7176d7a, 6a65e52, 0ff39b0..b9a22e5): a move's hits and riders on one creature are one number that counts up with each hurt and one line of the total; recoil and counters are one number on the attacker; status and turn-end damage stay one number per event, told apart by a new textless engine event `turn_end`; numbers stay `DAMAGE_NUMBER_MS` 4000 ms from their last change and leave with their creature when another enters; a counter shows with the hit that drew it, so a multi-hit's hits into a reflection follow each other; a move's blocked hits on one creature are one line; a missed move shows MISS on its target. Turns without counters, blocks or misses keep their exact planned time. [damage-display-2026-10-23](damage-display-2026-10-23.md) has the original's evidence (one `lostHP` per move, number at `isEnd`, 1.5 s), the rule and the checks.
