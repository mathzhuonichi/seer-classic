## User decisions (2026-10-21)

- Install only mechanics of the 2009-2013 mainland era; period sources beat modern articles, and a TW-only source is not enough.
- **Boss AI:** the 裸伤 model with the 奈尼芬多 exception. While an attack has PP, a useful status move 20% of the time; no useless status moves and no 0-damage attacks; finite-PP Bosses stand still at 0 PP.
- **Engine:** after a foe's switch, item or capsule our move is the first mover; the effect 40/30 doubling doubles damage, not power; fire is immune to burn and ice to freeze, like grass to 寄生; evasion shields (effects 78/106) no longer stop 必中 moves, while 圣洁 is unchanged.
- **Traits:** 免爆 gives a crit a 1/16 chance to deal 0; 反击类 trigger on any hit, status moves included; 回神 can trigger at 0 HP, not on damage over time; single-type boost traits do not boost dual-type moves; 虚无 dodges attacks only, not damage over time. Not approved: multiplicative 精准/回避, and 强攻 costing HP on a miss.
- Poison and burn keep ticking at the end of the turn.
- **Bosses:** 哈莫雷特 keeps its immunity and innate atk+2; add 米诺斯 (必中, unlimited PP, all stats +1 below half HP, our accuracy -2), 塔西亚 (a boost at 1/4 HP) and 麒麟·幻化之轮 (status immunity); 普兰特 refuses only stat drops from status moves, Wednesday's 厄尔塞拉 only accuracy drops.

## Coordinator decisions the user may override

- 必中 is the engine's one notion: the skill's accuracy is null, or a force-hit effect is active (`cond_sure_hit`, the Boss rule `always_hit`). 虚无 does not dodge 必中 attacks.
- 米诺斯 also gets status immunity and a 1/4 crit rate below half HP, from the same source sentence; its accuracy -2 applies to every player pet that enters, not only the lead.
- 塔西亚's quarter-HP boost is spa+2, labelled approximated: the source names no stat, and its attacks are special.
- Wednesday's 厄尔塞拉 also refuses burn and freeze, from the same source sentence.
- A dual type is immune when either type matches.
- 哈莫雷特's skill PP and 龙王圣殿's date stay; the evidence is recorded in the Boss data notes.

## Skill-effect catalog (paused, needs a user decision)

All 5,273 skill IDs in scope have an original SWF on the CDN, and 5,202 are exported. Publishing them as they are would not fix the 叶绿光束 report: 4,610 effects are drawn over the whole 960x560 stage, and the runtime squeezes each into a 350 px box at the target, so the beam becomes a thin line plus a translucent rectangle.

The placement research (decompiled `PetFightDLL_201308` `UseSkillController.petPlay`) found the original rule: the effect is placed at the attacker's registration point, unscaled, mirrored with the attacker, with the target at local (+600, 0) and the 960x560 stage clipping it. The options, with costs measured in the research:

- (a') original placement with the current 1,050 px frames: runtime change only, 1.85 GiB, soft on stage-sized effects at DPR 2;
- (b) re-export in stage space at 2.01 device px per stage px: about 5.1-5.5 GiB and 9 h with two jobs (7.4-8.2 GiB at 2.714);
- (c) Ruffle playing the original SWFs: 0.13 GB of assets but 15-29 MB of WASM, about 0.3 s per effect unless preloaded, and running the original ActionScript;
- (d) as (b), with full-stage tints stored as data.

The research recommends (a') now and (b) next. Its tools and data are in the session scratchpad, not in the repository. The export's own remaining steps: the failures 11064 and 11068, re-staging 658 candidates made by older exporter code, the real publish, the browser check and the design note's placeholders.
