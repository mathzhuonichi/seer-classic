## User decisions (2026-10-21)

- Install only mechanics of the 2009-2013 mainland era; period sources beat modern articles, and a TW-only source is not enough.
- **Boss AI:** the 裸伤 model with the 奈尼芬多 exception. While an attack has PP, a useful status move 20% of the time; no useless status moves and no 0-damage attacks; finite-PP Bosses stand still at 0 PP.
- **Engine:** after a foe's switch, item or capsule our move is the first mover; the effect 40/30 doubling doubles damage, not power; fire is immune to burn and ice to freeze, like grass to 寄生; evasion shields (effects 78/106) no longer stop 必中 moves, while 圣洁 is unchanged.
- **Traits:** 免爆 gives a crit a 1/16 chance to deal 0; 反击类 trigger on any hit, status moves included; 回神 can trigger at 0 HP, not on damage over time; single-type boost traits do not boost dual-type moves; 虚无 dodges attacks only, not damage over time. Not approved: multiplicative 精准/回避, and 强攻 costing HP on a miss.
- Poison and burn keep ticking at the end of the turn.
- **Bosses:** 哈莫雷特 keeps its immunity and innate atk+2; add 米诺斯 (必中, unlimited PP, all stats +1 below half HP, our accuracy -2), 塔西亚 (a boost at 1/4 HP) and 麒麟·幻化之轮 (status immunity); 普兰特 refuses only stat drops from status moves, Wednesday's 厄尔塞拉 only accuracy drops.

## Coordinator decisions the user may override

- 必中 is the engine's one notion: the skill's accuracy is null, or a force-hit effect is active (`cond_sure_hit`, the Boss rule `always_hit`). 虚无 does not dodge 必中 attacks.
- 米诺斯 also gets status immunity and a 1/4 crit rate below half HP, from the same source sentence; its accuracy -2 applies to every player pet that enters, not only the lead.
- 塔西亚's quarter-HP boost is spa+2, labelled approximated: the source names no stat, and its attacks are special.
- Wednesday's 厄尔塞拉 also refuses burn and freeze, from the same source sentence.
- A dual type is immune when either type matches.
- 哈莫雷特's skill PP and 龙王圣殿's date stay; the evidence is recorded in the Boss data notes.

## Skill effects: Ruffle (user decision 2026-10-22)

The user chose Ruffle (option c of the placement research): first a working version 1.0, then a redesign so that the player notices the loading as little as possible. Version 1.0 landed (1ded396..87c5027): all 5,273 in-scope skills play their original SWF in one Ruffle 0.7.1 host per battle screen, placed as `UseSkillController.petPlay` did, and the WebP path is gone. [skill-effects-ruffle-2026-10-22](skill-effects-ruffle-2026-10-22.md) has the placement rule, the loading boundary, the spike and in-situ measurements and the fidelity known issues (8 gradient-glow effects, 2 knockout inner glows, 13231, 12623; add/lighten/screen composite as normal). The paused WebP catalog export was discarded; its tree is backed up in the session scratchpad, and `tools/assets/skill_effect_sources.mjs` was kept.

The latency redesign is the next step: warm Ruffle before the first battle, never build a host while a battle is visible, time the effect against the original's frames and make no move wait for an effect that is not drawn.

## Open points

- **Boss AI:** the 20% draw can re-cast 药剂反噬 while it is still in place; after a faint replacement 奈尼芬多 probably re-casts one turn late; `boss-ai-isolation-v56` uses fixed seeds that a change in random draws can push out of bounds.
- **Move clips:** 10 sequences that teleport or swap their body now sit beyond the arena edge for a few frames (199, 261, 353, 426, 1181 attack; 468, 845, 883 sa; 1059 attack; 1122 cp); `sequencePaths` takes about 69 ms for 169 at clip load; 1327's attack and 50's status move have no hit marker.
- **Engine:** the `when.ts` fallback `turnOrder[0] === self.side` feeds AI estimates made before a turn's actions are known and does not anticipate a switch; the campaign 虚无封印 rule could read `ctx.sureHit` too.
- **Bosses:** 普兰特's 吸能 immunity and 麒麟·幻化之轮's exception for control moves are not modelled.
- **Unclear, not to change without period evidence:** first-mover knockouts and end-of-turn effects, simultaneous faints, multi-life Boss revives under multi-hit, wild monsters fleeing after n turns.
