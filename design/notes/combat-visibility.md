# Battle visibility follow-up

The battle now centers each creature's measured visible silhouette vertically, rather than anchoring every portrait to the bottom. Cards and exploration continue to use their original ground alignment. The arena fits a square animation host to the available space, retains evolution-relative sizes, and keeps shadows and ground effects attached to each creature's feet. On the desktop layout, health panels occupy the upper corners and the visible creature centers share the arena's middle line.

Each health panel now includes a compact strip of original offline creature portraits and the surviving/total count. The active creature has a colored ring, while defeated creatures are dimmed and marked. Every supplied encounter team member appears; there is no six-creature truncation of an opposing encounter. Both sides use the team state of the currently displayed animation frame, so a knockout, revival or replacement changes the icons at its corresponding event instead of exposing the resolved final state early.

Damage popups and logs display the full resolved hit: the actual HP loss plus the engine's existing overkill field. HP bars and damage-dependent mechanics continue to use actual loss.
