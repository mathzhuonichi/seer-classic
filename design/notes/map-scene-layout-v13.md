## Floor evidence and placement limits

`src/presentation/map-presentation.ts` contains compact static floor rectangles for 114 recovered H5 maps. These were computed offline from the official layout files in `.cache/map-assets/<id>/`. The official H5 `mapModel` module sets `_gridSize = 10` and constructs allowed points only for `road[x][y] === 1`; the client A-star implementation likewise treats truthy cells as traversable. Native image coordinates account for `bg_mc.x + bg_mc.offx` and `bg_mc.y + bg_mc.offy`, including the unusual registration of map 2. Exact asset paths, source URLs, hashes, and registration values are in `public/maps/original-manifest.json`.

Each computed rectangle stays inside open road cells, with an inset around its edges. Runtime receives four bounds, six distributed creature homes, and entry, NPC, boss, and exit anchors. It does not load road masks or introduce a grid-based pathfinding engine. The chosen rectangle is a conservative playable subset of the original road mask; it does not reconstruct the complete original map's traversal.

Nineteen other recovered scenes use visually placed floor rectangles because their H5 road masks are absent or unsuitable, including the twelve recovered Flash backgrounds. Room 4 and room 5 have explicit NPC and entry anchors. Map 25 has a manually spread stone-floor polygon; its six creature homes, player entry, and boss anchor were independently checked against open cells in the recovered mask. Maps 31 and 40 have broader manually placed polygons and staggered wildlife homes: the conservative source rectangles produced a crowded vertical strip in the ruin and tiny silhouettes on the ice field. Those broader polygons are visually chosen remake floors, not claims that every point is open in the original road mask. Helka's final entry is `(20,81)` and Cecilia's is `(24,74)`; their exits are separate from the wildlife homes. Those refinements, the visual floor rectangles, and all exit links remain explicit remake choices. Source images were inspected in the starter, Flash, and manual-anchor contact sheets under `.cache/map-assets/`.

The existing habitat list and `buildWorldPopulation` are unchanged: six ordinary creatures in populated habitats, singular scarce species, and no added wildlife in empty habitats. The presentation changes do not modify encounters, battle rules, rewards, or gameplay saves. Navigation preferences still retain region and position; older coordinates outside a new floor are displayed at the nearest floor edge.
