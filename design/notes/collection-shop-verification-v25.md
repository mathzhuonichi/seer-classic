# Independent collection and item-shop controller verification

`node tools/verification/collection-shop-v25.mjs` passes against the current controller. It creates an in-process Vite module loader with no listening preview server, uses isolated save fixtures, and does not modify the source catalog. Its machine-readable result is written to ignored `artifacts/collection-shop-v25/controller.json`.

The verifier independently derives directed ancestors from both forward evolution edges and `evolves_from` records for all 1,483 catalog species. A separate synthetic graph verifies reverse-only records, sibling exclusion, and termination in the presence of a cycle. Controller checks raise species 1 directly to Lv100 using pooled experience and the level editor, confirm immediate species 3 and collection credit for forms 1, 2, and 3, preserve actual monster count, and verify that previous snapshots remain unchanged. IV-only editing does not trigger evolution. Direct acquisition of advanced form 3 excludes unrelated starter and species-19-family credit. An old save containing advanced form 3 and omitting its middle ancestor is repaired immediately at startup and retains the repair through reload.

Shop checks cover all 51 ordinary source-catalog items, including zero stock, and all 1,196 defined or generated essence/bead products when present in inventory. An earned recruit essence is actually consumed through the hatching action; its zero-stock product remains unlocked and survives reload. A legacy cleared-boss record restores the corresponding consumed reward essence metadata. Purchases of 1 and 999 units succeed with zero coins and preserve RNG state and monsters. Previous snapshots retain their earlier product counts. Unearned recruit essence, unknown products, invalid quantities, safe-integer count overflow, and purchasing during an active battle all preserve the complete serialized save. The source catalog remains unchanged across controllers.

These checks verify controller transactions and catalog-derived relationships. They do not certify historical acquisition completeness or replace browser checks of the shop, laboratory target placement, or sidebar scrolling.
